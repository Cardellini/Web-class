Interview - IG Farm Attendant 


What do you do? 


We run a fish firm that collects fresh fish from fishermen every day and distributes them in various parts of Nairobi. 


Who is your target audience? 
We sell to retailers and small businesses like Mama Mboga’s, fish fryers, and hotels. We mainly focus on areas around Nairobi because fish need a lot of preservation, making long-distance deliveries hectic. Also, Nairobi is where cultures meet, and people are always looking to try new things. 


What information would you like your audience to see? 
I would like them to know who we are and what we do. If they could see the products and the various locations we deliver to, that would really help. They also need to have contact means and payment methods. Emphasis on payment before delivery. We operate on trust, and it is a system that works; it is Nairobi at the end of the day you know.


Any colors you want on the website? 
I would like it to be inspired by our colors, blue and yellow and also something that relates to fish and lake water. 


What do you hope people take from this?
They should understand what we do, how to reach us, and hopefully the call to action will get them to place an order,
