About IG Fish Farm — Gikomba Branch

IG Fish Farm supplies fresh tilapia to food businesses across Nairobi. We serve mama mbogas, food stalls, hotels and fish fryers with consistent quality and reliable supply.

Fresh From The Lake isn't just our slogan — it's our promise. We harvest daily and maintain a strict preservation process so your business receives the freshest fish possible.

Payment required before delivery. No order leaves our farm without full payment. This ensures we serve serious, established businesses who value quality tilapia. Trust and reliability is our foundation.
